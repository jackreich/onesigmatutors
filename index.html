Good to know.

Test prep for the ACT, SAT, GMAT, and GRE, plus math (Algebra I and II, Pre-calculus, Calculus I through III, Linear Algebra, and Differential Equations) and physics. Everything is one-on-one, and the same method, building content, strategy, and a study plan together, applies to each of them.

Start with a free 20-minute intro call. It can be with the student, with parents, or with both, together or separately, whatever works best. If it's a fit, I build a custom study plan around the student's goals and we get started.

No bulk plans and no long-term commitments. Sessions are pay-as-you-go, because every student's needs are different. You schedule what you need, when you need it.

Sessions are 30, 60, or 90 minutes, online, and one-on-one. You choose the length that fits the goal for the day, whether that's a focused drill or a full review of past work, new material, and a clear recap with next steps. We can work together from anywhere with the same materials and structure.

Between sessions, students also get a weekly 15-minute virtual office hours check-in. It's a quick chance to review progress, stay on track with the study plan, and ask a couple of technical questions so you're never stuck waiting until the next full session.
